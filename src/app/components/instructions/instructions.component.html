ChronoFlux Instructions ⚙️

ChronoFlux lets you create particles with customizable color, radius, and mass. You can define a custom mass unit and enable space mode 🪐 to simulate cosmic environments using special space bodies like planets, stars, and black holes.

🚀 Space Mode

When space mode is enabled, gravity and air resistance are automatically turned off to simulate a space vacuum. You can also manually turn off space, gravity, and air resistance to simulate a pure vacuum 🌌.

⏱️ Simulation Settings

You can adjust the simulation speed using Delta Time. Lower values slow things down 🐢, while higher values speed things up 🚀. You can also change the wall elasticity to control how particles bounce off walls.

🎯 Custom Forces

Add your own forces by setting X and Y components, giving them a name, and choosing a color 🎨. These forces can be applied to all particles in the simulation.

💥 Pulse Points

Create a Pulse Point that can be triggered manually or at intervals. This simulates a blast-like force in a specific area.

🌀 Centripetal Force

Enable Centripetal (Planetary) Force to simulate orbital motion. Choose a static planet to act as a center, and allow other bodies to orbit around it. You can also define a custom gravitational constant (G) 🌍.

🕹️ Controls

Click Turn On to start the simulation ▶️. You can pause it at any time ⏸️. Use Clear Particles to remove all particles from the board 🧹.

To delete a specific particle, hold Shift and double-click on the canvas. You can also delete particles from the Particles section below the diagram.

📊 Particles Section

This section contains three parts:

  • Particle Diagram – shows particle positions and their force vectors in 2D space.
  • Properties Panel – lets you view and change mass, mass unit, and radius in real-time.
  • Forces Info – shows force angles and magnitudes applied to each particle.
